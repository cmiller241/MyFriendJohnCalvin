How it works

1. Click a frame on the timeline.
2. Drag a character on the stage. That frame becomes a keyframe ◆.
3. Click a later frame and move it again.
4. Press Space to play. The frames in between are drawn for you.

Drag to move · Scroll to resize · Shift+scroll to rotate · Ctrl+scroll to zoom
